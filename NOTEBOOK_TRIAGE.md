## Recommendations

1. Keep the five CANONICAL notebooks as current notebook-level diagnostics/figure implementations. Re-run inspect_final.ipynb against promoted final objects when fresh visual outputs are wanted; its saved outputs predate annotation migration.
2. Keep the eleven PIPELINE-DEPENDED public DRAFT notebooks and manifest together until publication review decides whether to remove DRAFT_. If promoted, update filenames, manifest, and reports aggregate in one coordinated change.
3. Treat 90 STALE files as cleanup candidates, not automatic deletion authority. The safest first tranche is 37 checkpoints plus empty/near-empty Untitled notebooks. Preserve historical decision notebooks until the paper audit no longer needs them.
4. Do not recover annotation calls from a notebook when a CSV disagrees. Resolve CSV/spec/vocabulary first, regenerate presentation notebooks from it, then re-render.
5. Port Figure 3c into workflow/scripts/figures.py before downgrading that notebook from CANONICAL.
