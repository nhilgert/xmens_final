  discriminative panel. The JSON cosines and the saved-matrix cosines are different quantities
  (different panel + centring); each is verified against its own file.

  on the human deliverable (X_scVI neighbors, seed 0) — a finer sibling of the leiden-0.5 arm.
  Exports beside the driver JSON: `hammoud_h_cluster_assignments.csv` (47,533 barcodes, verified
  10/10 against JSON counts), `hammoud_driver_computation.py` (the verbatim producing script), and
  `hammoud_h_provenance.txt`. The combined notebook §4 now cross-tabulates h ↔ leiden-0.5 with
  verifies (h9 → cluster 5; h2/h6/h7 → cluster 1; h5 holds 98% of cluster 3). Cross-species driver
  claims stay keyed on Stro subtype (granularity differs between species' clusterings).
- **Driver-JSON panel provenance also recovered:** an atlas expression-floor panel (atlas genes
  with max > 0.5 across the 6 fib columns; human n=3,099, mouse n=2,728 via human-unique 1:1
  orthologs), centring over the 6 fib columns, `frac_from_top30` = top-30 contribution sum / total
  cosine. NOT a variance panel — which is why the extras notebook's two reconstructions differ
  from it by design.
- **Prose drift found and corrected during compilation** (old numbers must not be quoted): C7 block
  is 14.4× / 61.0% / 9.8% / 5.60% (was 16.2× / 58.2% / 7.9% / 6.63%); Stro-7 is reciprocal at all
